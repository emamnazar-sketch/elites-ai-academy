Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 20, 2026

Elites AI Academy is an online course that teaches everyday AI skills. This page explains, in plain words, what information we keep about you, why we keep it, and the choices you have. If anything here is unclear, email us at support@elitesaiacademy.com.

Our promise, first and plain: we never sell, rent, trade, or share our students' data with anyone. Not with advertisers. Not with partners. Not with other companies. Your information stays with your account, and nowhere else.

What we collect

  • Your name and email address — you give these when you create an account.
  • If you sign in with Google — Google shares your name and email address with us so we can create and open your account. We never see your Google password.
  • Your password, if you set one — it is stored scrambled (hashed), never as plain text. Nobody at Elites AI Academy can read it.
  • Your course progress — which lessons you have finished, so you can pick up where you left off on any device.

That is the whole list. We do not collect your payment card details on this site, and we do not buy information about you from anyone.

Cookies

We use one cookie that keeps you signed in for up to 30 days, and a short-lived cookie that keeps the Google sign-in steps safe. We do not use advertising cookies or tracking cookies.

How we use your information

  • To run your account and sign you in.
  • To save your progress.
  • To answer you when you email us for help.
  • To keep the site safe and working.

We do not sell your personal information. We do not share it with advertisers, data brokers, or anyone else.

Protected to the HIPAA standard

Elites AI Academy is a school, not a health service, and we never collect health information. Even so, we protect student data to the same strict standard that HIPAA sets for health records:

  • Your information is encrypted while it travels to us and while it is stored.
  • Access is limited — your data is only used to run your account, never browsed or passed around.
  • Passwords and sign-in tokens are stored scrambled (hashed), so even our own database never holds them in readable form.
  • There are no advertising trackers and no third-party analytics on this site watching what you do.

Your student record

Your course progress is your private student record, and we treat it the way education privacy law (FERPA) treats school records:

  • Only you can see your record. Other students cannot.
  • We do not disclose your record to employers, schools, family members, or anyone else without your written permission.
  • You can review your record anytime in the portal, ask us to correct anything wrong in it, and have it deleted with your account.

The systems that store it

Two services store your information only so the site can run: Cloudflare hosts the website and the account database, and Google provides the optional Google sign-in. They process it to operate the site — they do not get to use it for their own purposes, and nothing is shared with them beyond what the sign-in and hosting need. The only other exception is the law itself: if a court order ever forces a disclosure, we would have to comply, and we would tell you unless the order forbids it.

Your choices and your rights

  • You can see your name and email, and fix your name, on your Profile page after you sign in.
  • Email us at support@elitesaiacademy.com and we will correct your information, or delete your account and everything attached to it. Deleting your account removes your name, email, and progress for good.
  • Under California privacy law (CCPA/CPRA) and similar laws elsewhere, you also have the right to know exactly what we hold about you and to get a copy of it. We do not sell personal information, so there is nothing to opt out of — the answer is already no, for everyone, always.
  • We keep your information only while your account is active. When an account is deleted, its information is deleted with it.

Children

Elites AI Academy is made for adults. It is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly keep accounts for children under 13.

Keeping your information safe

Sign-ins travel over a secure, encrypted connection, data is stored encrypted, and passwords are stored scrambled. No website can promise perfect security, but we keep what we collect to the small list above, which limits the risk. If a security problem ever affects your information, we will email the affected students, explain what happened, and say what we did about it.

Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we will update this page and the date at the top. Big changes will also be announced on the site.
